About

I'm a Senior Product Manager with a background spanning developer platforms, internal tools, content management systems, and API platforms. I've spent my career thinking about how to make complex software products feel simple and useful.

Earlier in my career I worked in product innovation, building prototypes at the edge of what was possible — VR and AR experiences, payments in new environments like car operating systems, and other bets on where technology was heading.

Right now I'm most interested in AI. I believe we're at a moment where a PM with curiosity and the right tools can ship real products faster than ever before. This site is where I'm putting that to the test.
